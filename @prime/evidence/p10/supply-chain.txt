## Fixes and decisions
- pulldown-cmark 0.13.4 -> 0.13.5 (RUSTSEC-2026-0414). polaris-ingest parsed Markdown with Options::all(), which enables
  wikilinks: an ingested document of [[a|]] links could make parsing grow exponentially. Fixed by the upgrade and by
  disabling wikilinks in ingest (regression test wikilink_bombs_parse_as_plain_text).
- crypto-bigint 0.5.5 (RUSTSEC-2026-0387) accepted with reason: reached only via biscuit-auth's always-compiled P-256
  support; Polaris creates, signs and parses Ed25519 keys only. The fix needs crypto-bigint 0.7, which elliptic-curve 0.13
  cannot use.
- six unmaintained transitive crates accepted with reasons (paste via leptos, rustls-pemfile via the OpenShell SDK,
  ttf-parser via pdf-extract/ab_glyph, zvariant/zvariant_derive/zbus_names via Slint's accesskit on Linux).
  All ignores are in deny.toml with their reasons.
