    ├ Advisory: https://rustsec.org/advisories/RUSTSEC-2026-0368
    ├ In `zip` 9.0.0-pre2, `ZipArchive::extract` creates a symbolic link from an archive entry after validating only the output entry path.       For further details see: https://github.com/zip-rs/zip2/security/advisories/GHSA-4v9h-p7m9-rh26
