## Bug found and fixed during the smoke
RemoteSession::next_record took a record off the stream and then awaited read_text; when the
frontend's select! picked another branch meanwhile (the page's GET /api/sessions right after a
submit), the record was dropped and the message never appeared live. It now keeps the record in
a pending slot (as OfflineSession does). Regression test
session/remote.rs::a_dropped_next_record_never_skips_a_message fails on the old code
(left: [], right: ["first", "second", "third"]) and passes on the fix.
